    About

    Segmentation by HD-GLIO (Kickingereder, Isensee et al., Lancet Oncol 2019) with brain extraction by HD-BET (Isensee et al., Hum Brain Mapp 2019), both from Heidelberg University Hospital and DKFZ, run here with ONNX Runtime. Preprocessing follows the HD-GLIO instructions: reorientation to standard, HD-BET brain mask, 6-parameter registration to T1, masking.

    Network weights are stored as 8-bit integers per output channel and restored to 32-bit at load. On the demo case this changed fewer than 0.01% of voxels against the original PyTorch models (see VERIFICATION.md). HD-GLIO weights: CC BY 4.0. HD-BET weights: CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial use only.

    Optional add-on model files: nnU-Net BraTS 2020 (Isensee et al. 2021), Raidionics FLAIR hyperintensity (Faanes, Bouget et al. 2026), Raidionics tumor core (Bouget et al. 2026), AURORA brain metastases (Buchner et al. 2023) and vestibular schwannoma (Kujawa et al. 2024), each run with its own published preprocessing; see the README.

    Research software, not a medical device. Not for clinical decision-making.
